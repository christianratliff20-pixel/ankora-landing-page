The Platform

Core operations meet total AI control.

Ankora handles your day-to-day scheduling, dispatching, and call answering automatically—while giving you deep, granular control over how the AI behaves so it never becomes a hazard.

24/7 Call Handling

Answers every ring instantly with natural voice cadence. Never dead air—if it mishears, it politely asks again instead of breaking down.

Calendar & Booking Sync

Cross-checks your availability in milliseconds. Books, reschedules, or takes detailed messages while confirming every detail before hanging up.

Training Sandbox & Natural Testing

Train your AI using natural language just like Claude or ChatGPT. Run mock sessions and stress-test edge cases before it ever speaks to a real client.

Granular Autonomy Toggles

Switch actions between manual, ask-first, and full-auto. Make routine booking automatic, but force the AI to require your approval for custom quotes.

Live Monitoring & "Whisper" Intercept

Watch transcripts scroll live. Peek into active calls, whisper instructions to the AI mid-call, or fluidly take over the line whenever needed.

Full Lead Intelligence

Get structured summaries, complete transcripts, and caller metrics instantly delivered so you never lose track of a high-value opportunity.

Your Rules

You decide what it's allowed to do.

Most platforms act however they were shipped. Ankora puts every action on a dial you control: off, ask-first, or full-auto — per action, per operating mode, per teammate.

⚓ Per-action autonomy

Booking, transfers, callbacks — each one set to off, confirm-with-you-first, or fully automatic. Change it anytime.

⚓ Operating modes

Normal, after-hours, vacation, or build your own. Scheduled modes revert automatically when you're back.

⚓ Roles your way

Create custom roles for your team and check off exactly which pages and actions each one can touch.

⚓ Your data, always yours

One-click export of everything — calls, transcripts, knowledge base. Easy to leave is how you know you can trust staying.
